On-court training

Skills are built in practice. Confidence is built in play.

Our training emphasizes movement, rally skills, technique, and decision-making through engaging drills and point play. Players are challenged at a level that supports their development.

Competitive growth matters, but so do sportsmanship, friendships, enjoyment, and the confidence to keep improving.

Our philosophy

Good tennis. Stronger people. Lasting community.

Canyon Trail Tennis is led by a coach with experience across youth sports, school athletics, and community college tennis. We believe strong programs combine thoughtful instruction with meaningful opportunities to participate and compete.

Whether a player is learning to rally or preparing for a competitive match, our goal is to help them feel challenged, supported, and connected to a team.
